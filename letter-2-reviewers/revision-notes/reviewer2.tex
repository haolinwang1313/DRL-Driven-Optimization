\begin{ReviewerComment}
This paper studies a DRL-DNN framework for block-scale co-optimization of urban morphology and energy performance, using a surrogate model and DDPG agents to search for improved design strategies. The topic is interesting, relevant, and worth investigating. The overall structure of the manuscript is clear, and the figures help communicate the workflow and results. However, several important issues should be addressed before the paper can be considered for publication.
\end{ReviewerComment}
%
\begin{Answer}
We sincerely thank the Reviewer for the careful and constructive comments. We especially appreciate the emphasis on methodological positioning, benchmark fairness, surrogate reliability, and the limits of the current validation mode. These comments identified several places where the previous manuscript was not yet sufficiently balanced. In the revised manuscript, the paper no longer claims that DDPG outperforms NSGA-II. Instead, it presents a selected-2000-sample comparison in which fair-budget NSGA-II remains stronger than all three DDPG scenarios, and it interprets that outcome through checkpoint sensitivity, bounded physical probing, and explicit claim limits. We address each point below.

To ensure that the revision is visible at the level of substance rather than only at the level of wording, we also reorganized the manuscript around a clearer evidence structure. This includes the dataset-scale selection logic in Appendix~A, the selected-surrogate benchmark figures and budget accounting in the main text, and the checkpoint-sensitivity, reevaluation, and physical-probe diagnostics in Appendix~B.
\end{Answer}

\begin{ReviewerComment}
The DRL framework is interesting, but the formulation should be justified more carefully.
