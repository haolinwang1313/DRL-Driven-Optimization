Dear Editor,\par
\vspace{0.7em}
We sincerely thank the editor and reviewers for their careful reading of our manuscript and for the constructive comments. The comments helped us identify several places where the original manuscript did not sufficiently explain the data-generation workflow, the sampling strategy, the DDPG formulation, the optimizer output contracts, the physical checking procedure, and the climate boundary of the study. We have therefore revised the manuscript substantially rather than making only local edits.\par
\vspace{0.7em}
The revised manuscript now explicitly distinguishes generated feasible block morphologies, morphology descriptors, analytic response generation, DNN surrogate prediction, descriptor-space optimization, nearest-feasible-block projection, physics-based cross-model stress testing, and cross-climate sensitivity analysis. We also reorganized the Supplementary Information to provide the response-generation equations, descriptor-dependency checks, surrogate validation results, optimizer-comparison details, projection analysis, physical stress-test outputs, and climate-sensitivity tables.\par
\vspace{0.7em}
The main revisions are summarized in Table R1. We then provide point-by-point responses to all reviewer comments. In each response, we first address the reviewer's concern, then summarize the corresponding manuscript and Supplementary Information revisions, and finally state the interpretation boundary where appropriate.\par
