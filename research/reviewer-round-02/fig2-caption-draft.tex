Figure 2. Serialized surrogate-query process used in the DDPG
benchmark. The normalized three-target state is mapped by the actor to
an absolute 12-dimensional descriptor query. After Gaussian exploration
and clipping, the query is evaluated by the guarded DNN surrogate,
which returns the next state and normalized-distance reward. Each
episode begins from a random descriptor query and terminates after 40
surrogate interactions. The sequence represents repeated black-box
queries rather than physical time evolution; the actor--critic learning
mechanics are shown in Fig. 3.
