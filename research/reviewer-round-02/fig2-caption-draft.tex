Figure 2. Workflow of the DDPG-based surrogate search. The optimization
starts by initializing the surrogate-based environment and selecting one
preference scenario. Each episode begins from a random descriptor query
and then proceeds through repeated surrogate-query steps, in which the
actor produces an absolute descriptor query and the guarded surrogate
returns the next state and reward. The episode terminates after a fixed
query horizon, and the process continues until all episodes and seeds are
completed.
