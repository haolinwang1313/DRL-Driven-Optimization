Figure 3. Actor--critic architecture and learning equations used in the
DDPG implementation. The online actor maps the three-dimensional state
to a 12-dimensional normalized descriptor action, and the online critic
evaluates the resulting state--action pair. Target copies provide the
temporal-difference target and are updated softly using factor
\(\tau\). The right panel summarizes the temporal-difference target,
critic loss, actor objective, and target-network updates. Here,
\(\mu\) denotes the actor policy, \(Q\) the action-value function,
primed symbols the target networks, \(d_i\) the terminal indicator, and
\(N\) the mini-batch size.
