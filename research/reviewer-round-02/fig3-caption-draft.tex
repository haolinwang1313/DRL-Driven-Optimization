Figure 3. Actor--critic architecture and parameter updates used in the
DDPG implementation. A replay mini-batch provides the normalized
three-target state, absolute normalized morphology-descriptor action,
reward, next state, and terminal indicator. The online actor maps the
three-dimensional state to a 12-dimensional descriptor action, while
the online critic evaluates the state--action pair. The target networks
construct the temporal-difference target. The online networks are
updated through the actor objective and critic mean-squared-error loss,
and the target parameters are updated softly using factor
\(\tau\). Solid arrows denote forward tensor or data flow, dashed arrows
denote gradient-based optimizer updates, and dash-dotted arrows denote
soft target-network updates. Here, \(\mu\) denotes the actor policy,
\(Q\) denotes the action-value function, \(\gamma\) denotes the discount
factor, \(\tau\) denotes the soft-update factor, \(N\) denotes the
mini-batch size, and \(\theta,\theta'\) denote online and target
parameters.
